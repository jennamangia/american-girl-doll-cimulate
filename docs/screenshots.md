# Screenshot provenance

All American Girl image assets derive from the supplied ZIP. The current search captures are byte-for-byte copies.

| Query                  | Illustrative source and assembly                                                                                                                                              |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Beauty and the Beast   | `beauty and the best ACS.png`, used as supplied                                                                                                                               |
| Black dress            | `black dress ACS 1.png` contributes two tiles. `ACS 2` through `ACS 5` contribute one tile each. The six tiles form a three-column grid in reading order.                     |
| Doll for my 8 year old | `doll for my 8 year old ACS 1.png` contributes Felicity, Samantha, Molly, Addy, Josefina, and Kaya. Two promotional panels were removed. `ACS 2` contributes Kirsten and Kit. |
| Puppy for my doll      | `puppy for my doll ACS.png` contributes all eight tiles. The dark outer border and source dividers were removed.                                                              |

The rebuilt dress, doll, and puppy grids use equal-size cards and single-pixel dividers. Tile contents retain their original pixels and proportions. Product photography, visible text, pricing, and native tile styling come from the original captures. The source screenshots truncate some product titles.

West Marine, PacSun, and Ann Taylor screenshots and the general reported customer-success metrics come from the Ariat reference project.

The Shopper Agent uses a fresh browser capture of the same [Kit Kittredge product page](https://www.americangirl.com/products/kit-kittredge-18-inch-doll-book-accessories-historical-characters-jmh91) shown in the brief. The inline image was visible in the brief but unavailable as a downloadable file. The capture preserves the retailer’s page as rendered, including the selected store and availability at capture time. The WebP conversion is lossless. The accompanying chat is scripted.
